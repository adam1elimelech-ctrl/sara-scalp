Burn scar camouflage — medical tattooing in Jerusalem

A burn often leaves mixed pale and darker patches and uneven texture. Once the tissue is stable, missing colour can be rebuilt in thin layers.

We do not promise the burn disappears. The goal is that the eye no longer jumps to the patch. Assessment is in daylight at Hamor 1.

Why a burn scar is not a surgical line

A surgical scar is usually one line. A burn is a field: light islands, darker ridges, changing thickness. Each zone is judged on its own.

That is why burn work is slower and often needs more sessions than a caesarean line.

When burn camouflage may start

The burn must be closed, quiet and not in an active graft phase. Fresh burns stay with the medical team. We tattoo settled tissue only.

What a burn consult looks like

Daylight mapping of pale versus dark zones, sensation, and what you already tried. We say which parts can take pigment and which should be left.

What happens in the session

Slow work. Breaks if the area is sensitive. Thin layers in the pale zones — not a wash over the whole burn.

How many sessions

Usually several, weeks apart, set by size and how the tissue holds pigment — not by a slogan.

After treatment

The site looks darker first, then softens. Do not pick. Itch is common; scratching pulls colour out. Follow the sheet from the room.

Burns on different sites

Arm, neck, chest and face have different sun and clothing. A neck burn in Jerusalem needs a sun plan after the skin closes.

What you already tried

Creams, silicone, laser and grafts can help texture or tightness. They do not always refill missing colour. That leftover contrast is what we assess.

Who is not suitable now

Open areas, active thickening, infection, or a burn still changing month to month.

Privacy and photos

Your photos stay for advice unless you agree otherwise.

How to book in Jerusalem

WhatsApp or email. Where the burn is, when it happened, and a daylight photo.

After a skin graft

Grafted skin behaves differently. We wait until the graft is quiet and we do not treat it like a simple surgical line.

Scald, oil and flame

The cause matters less than how the tissue looks now. Mixed colour fields are common after all three.

Sensation, itch and local heat

Some burns are numb; some are oversensitive. Both change how a session feels. Itch in healing is expected — not a licence to scratch.

Sun, pools and a Jerusalem summer

Chlorine and strong sun wait. After closure, exposed burns need high SPF. This city bleaches contrast back quickly.

Children, teens and adults

We treat adults who decide for themselves. A minor is not booked here.

How we talk about results

Less contrast. Not ‘gone’. Close-up honesty beats a before-and-after slogan.

Questions worth bringing

Which zones will be left alone. How many layers. What sun does. What if the first layer looks too dark.

Why a clinic, not a standard tattoo studio

Burn tissue is not a decorative canvas. Colour matching to surrounding skin is a different skill from drawing a design.

Before you message

Stable burn. Mixed map. Layers. Honest limits. Hamor 1.

Clothes, work and the sea

Most desk work can resume quickly. Sweaty or dirty work needs a plan. Sea and pools after the surface has closed.

Dark and light colour in the same burn

We fill missing colour. We do not bleach dark patches with pigment. Two problems in one field may need two kinds of answer.

Medicines, health and smoking

Tell us what you take and any healing problems. Smoking can slow how skin holds work. We do not give medical advice in place of your doctor.

If you dislike the first layer

First layers are conservative on purpose. Wait for the fade before judging. We correct at the next visit — we do not pile dark pigment on day two.

Travelling to Jerusalem

Build time for consult and later sessions. Do not book a beach week on top of the first healing days.

Follow-up after a series

We look at settled colour in the same daylight. A later refresh is possible. It is not a failure of the first series.

Before the form

If the burn is still changing, write that. Better a delayed start than a session on the wrong tissue.

Burn work versus ordinary scar camouflage

Same family of technique. Different map, sensation and session count. Do not copy aftercare from a caesarean group and apply it to a burn.

What to write in the first message

Where. When. What you already tried. One or two daylight photos.
