Scalp micropigmentation (SMP)

Tiny dots on the scalp that read as follicles in a short-crop look. We do not implant hair.

מדריך

SMP in Jerusalem — what we check first

Skin on the scalp, existing hair, the hairline you actually want, and whether you accept a cropped look.

Colour that stays believable

Too-black dots age badly. We mix for the scalp in front of us, not for a photo filter.

A scar on the scalp

Scar tissue takes dots differently. It may need its own passes.

Upkeep

Sun on a bare scalp in Jerusalem fades work. SPF after the skin closes. A refresh after years is common.

What SMP does not do

It does not grow hair. It does not hide a long style. It does not replace a transplant if length is the goal.
