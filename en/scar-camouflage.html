Scar camouflage · Jerusalem

Scar camouflage with medical tattooing

Pale scars after surgery, a cut or an injury can be softened by matching pigment to the surrounding skin. Consultation at Hamor 1.

The work

We restore colour. We do not draw over the scar.

Scar camouflage is paramedical tattooing: pigment placed in thin layers so the pale line is less obvious in ordinary light. It is not a decorative cover-up.

Red or active scars wait. A white scar that has settled — often 12–18 months after surgery — is the usual candidate.

Book a consultation

Who it may suit

  • Caesarean or tummy-tuck scars
  • Breast or orthopaedic scars
  • Pale injury lines that have matured

Questions

Will the scar vanish?

No. Contrast drops. Texture may still be felt. That is said before we start.

How many visits?

Usually several layers. Colour settles between sessions.

Guide

Scar camouflage in Jerusalem — what to know

We match pigment to the live skin next to the scar, in daylight — not to a photo from the internet.

Belt lines, bra bands and waistbands rub. Plan loose clothing for the first days.

When we wait

Red scars, keloid tendency, or skin still changing after a recent operation. Waiting is part of the service.
