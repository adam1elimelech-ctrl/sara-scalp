Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making the website and services accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Commitment to Accessibility

Sara Scalp – Medical Tattoo Clinic is committed to making its website and digital services accessible to the entire public, including people with disabilities, in accordance with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adaptations), 2013.

The website aims to comply with Israeli Standard 5568 (based on WCAG 2.0 / 2.1 Level AA guidelines).

Accessibility Adaptations Implemented

  • Clear semantic structure with hierarchical headings (H1–H3)
  • Full keyboard navigation
  • Alternative text (alt) for images (to be completed by the website editor for every new image)
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background
  • Forms with clear labels
  • Clear links and descriptions
  • Support for common screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver)
  • Responsive design suitable for mobile and different devices

Note for website editor: Ensure every new image receives descriptive alt text, add a skip-to-content link, check contrast, and verify that every interactive element is keyboard-accessible. A professional accessibility audit is recommended; update this statement accordingly.

Known Accessibility Limitations

The website is in ongoing development and accessibility improvement. Temporary limitations may exist in some components (e.g., images without full alt text, or third-party elements). We work to correct issues continuously. If you encounter a problem – please report it to us.
