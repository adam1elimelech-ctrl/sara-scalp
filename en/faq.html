Frequently Asked Questions – Before & After Treatment

Important information for all medical tattoo fields at Sara Scalp

Important: The treatments are aesthetic / paramedical and do not replace medical consultation or treatment. For any medical question – consult a physician.

General – Before Any Treatment

When is the scar / area ready for treatment? +
Usually at least 12–18 months after surgery / injury / burn, when the area is stable, without active redness, itching or unusual sensitivity. In the consultation we will check the specific case.
Do I need a doctor’s approval? +
In certain cases (medical conditions, medications, pregnancy/breastfeeding, tendency to keloids) we will recommend medical clearance. Always mention medical conditions in the consultation.
What to bring / how to prepare? +
Arrive with clean skin (no heavy creams on the area). Avoid strong sun exposure in the days before. Tell us about medications, allergies and skin conditions.
How painful is it? +
Most clients describe mild to moderate discomfort. A numbing cream can be used. Scars are sometimes less sensitive than normal skin.

Scar Camouflage

Before: Which scars are suitable? +
Relatively flat, stable scars that are lighter than the surrounding skin. Hypertrophic/keloid scars require individual assessment.
After: What to do at home? +
Keep the area clean, avoid direct sun, do not rub, use recommended products, avoid pools/sea for the first 2–3 days. Follow the exact instructions you received.

Burn Scar Camouflage

Before: When can treatment begin? +
Only when the burn has fully matured and the area is stable. Assessment of depth and type of burn is required.
After: How many sessions are expected? +
Usually 2–5, depending on size and condition. Between sessions the color stabilizes and we adjust the continuation.

Hypopigmentation

Before: Is it suitable for vitiligo? +
Only in stable cases and with individual assessment (sometimes with dermatologist consultation). Not every case is suitable.
After: When is the final result visible? +
After the color stabilizes (weeks to months after the series). Follow the home-care instructions.

Stretch Marks & Birthmarks

Before: When are stretch marks ready? +
When they are stable (usually white/silvery) and not actively red/purple.
After: Is the result perfect? +
The improvement is significant, but not always 100% – it is living skin. Expectations are set in the consultation.

Natural Freckles & Cheek Enhancement

Before: How is the look chosen? +
In the consultation we match density, shade and placement according to facial structure and your preference – always with delicacy.
After: How long does it last? +
Months to years, depending on skin and sun exposure. Refresh possible as needed.

Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)

Before: Suitable after hair transplant? +
Yes, often – for filling or improving density. We will check the situation in the consultation.
After: How to maintain the result? +
Avoid strong sun and aggressive washing in the first days. The result is stable for years with possible refresh.

General – After Any Treatment

  • Strictly follow the home-care instructions you received
  • Avoid direct sun, pools, saunas and extreme effort in the first days
  • Do not rub or pick
  • In case of unusual redness, prolonged swelling or concern – contact us immediately
  • Attend follow-up sessions according to the plan
