Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)

The appearance of short, dense cropped hair on the scalp – a professional solution for hair loss

Book an SMP Consultation

Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP) is an advanced micropigmentation technique that creates the illusion of short, dense hair on the scalp. Through tiny pigment dots implanted into the upper layers of the skin, the appearance of hair follicles is achieved – exactly like short cropped hair. At Sara Scalp in Jerusalem we perform SMP at the highest level, with a team trained in the technique in Brazil and Turkey.

Who Is SMP Suitable For?

  • Men with male pattern baldness (Androgenetic Alopecia)
  • Women with thinning hair or hair loss
  • Anyone who has undergone a hair transplant and wants to fill areas or improve density
  • Anyone with scalp scars who wants to camouflage them
  • Anyone who prefers the look of short, clean cropped hair

SMP is a non-invasive solution, without surgery, and without the need for daily maintenance such as medications or products. The result looks natural even up close when performed correctly – with adapted density, precise tone, and a natural hairline.

How Is the Treatment Performed?

The process begins with a comprehensive consultation in which we evaluate the condition of the scalp, the pattern of hair loss, and the desired look. Then tone matching is performed (it is very important that the color is not too dark), and planning of the hairline and density. The work itself is done over several sessions – usually 2–4 – in order to build the density gradually and ensure a natural result.

Between sessions the color stabilizes and we see how it integrates. This allows correction and improvement until the result is perfect. After treatment avoid strong sun, excessive sweating and harsh washing of the scalp in the first days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does SMP look like a tattoo? +
When the work is done correctly – no. It looks like short cropped hair. The secret is in the tone, density and dot technique.
How long do the results last? +
Several years. Gradual fading may occur, and a refresh can be performed as needed.
Can SMP be done after a hair transplant? +
Yes, often. SMP can fill areas or improve the overall appearance after a transplant.
