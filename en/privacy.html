Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. General

This Privacy Policy applies to the use of the Sara Scalp website (the “Website”) and the services offered through it. We respect users’ privacy and are committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Privacy Protection Law, 1981 (including amendments), the Privacy Protection Regulations (Data Security), and any relevant law.

Use of the Website constitutes acceptance of this Policy. If you do not agree – please refrain from using the Website and from providing information.

2. What Information Do We Collect?

Information you provide voluntarily:

  • Full name, phone number, email address – when filling out the appointment form or contacting us
  • Additional details you choose to provide (notes, description of the area to be treated, etc.)

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, time of visit
  • Cookies for website functionality, analytics and experience improvement (if activated)

3. Purposes of Use

  • Responding to inquiries and booking appointments
  • Contacting you to confirm appointments, send reminders and continue treatment
  • Improving the service and the Website
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Direct marketing (only with explicit consent, in accordance with the Law)

We will not transfer your information to third parties for marketing purposes without your consent, except for essential service providers (such as SMS / scheduling services) acting on our behalf and subject to confidentiality commitments.

4. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to secure the information. Information will be retained for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law. Afterwards it will be deleted or anonymized.

5. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Protection Law you have the following rights (among others):

  • Right to access your personal information
  • Right to correction or deletion of incorrect, incomplete or outdated information
  • Right to object to certain uses (subject to the Law)
  • Right to withdraw consent to marketing

To exercise your rights – contact us in writing at the email or phone below.
