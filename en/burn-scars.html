Burn scar camouflage

Once burn tissue has settled, missing colour can be rebuilt in thin layers. The aim is less contrast — not a promise that the burn disappears.

How it differs

A burn is not a surgical line

Burn scars often mix pale and darker patches with uneven texture. We assess the map in daylight at Hamor 1 before any needle work. Fresh, open or unstable tissue is not treated.

Pigment is placed only where colour is missing. We do not paint the whole area as if it were a canvas.

Book a consultation

Who it may suit

  • Fully healed burn scars
  • Pale islands inside an older burn
  • Colour contrast that remains after creams or laser

Questions

Is every burn suitable?

No. Recent burns, active areas or a keloid tendency are deferred or referred.

How many sessions?

Usually several, weeks apart, depending on size and how the skin holds pigment.

Guide

What burn scar camouflage can and cannot do

Paramedical tattooing fills missing colour. It does not rebuild elasticity or flatten raised tissue. If texture is the main problem, we say so at consultation.

Expect the area to look darker for days, then soften. Judge the result after healing, not the same evening.

Before you come in

The burn must be closed and quiet. Tell us about grafts, recent laser and any tendency to thicken. Wear loose clothing over the site.

Sun on a fresh tan confuses colour matching. Avoid heavy tanning on the area in the weeks before.

Aftercare in brief

Clean hands. A thin layer of the ointment we give you. Do not pick flakes. Keep chlorine, saunas and hard workouts off the site in the first weeks.

Jerusalem sun on arms and neck will bring the contrast back if you skip protection once the skin has closed.
