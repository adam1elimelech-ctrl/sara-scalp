Birthmark camouflage

A mark you were born with is not erased with paint. We check type, stability and site, then decide if contrast can be softened.

Not every birthmark belongs in a tattoo chair

Flat, unchanged marks may be candidates. Raised, changing or bleeding marks go to a doctor first. Dark marks are not simply painted light.

Face work is slower and sometimes declined if the risk of an odd tone is higher than the benefit.
