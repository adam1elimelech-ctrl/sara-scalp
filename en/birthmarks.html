Honest limits

Contrast drops. Someone who knows the spot may still see it close up.

After the session

Same family of aftercare: no picking, delayed swimming, sun plan on exposed sites.

Privacy and price

Photos stay private unless you agree. Cost follows area and sessions after we see you.

What people say versus what matters

Stories about school or beach are real. The medical fact is whether the mark is flat and quiet.

Why we do not flood colour to make it ‘vanish’

Overfill looks painted. Understated layers age better.

Work, sea and daily life

After healing, live as usual with sun sense on neck and arms.
