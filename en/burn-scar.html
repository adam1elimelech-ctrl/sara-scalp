Burn Scar Camouflage with Medical Tattoo

Restoring a more even appearance to burn scars through precise and professional tone matching

Book a Burn Scar Consultation

Burn scars are among the most challenging aesthetically. They often remain white, shiny, or with a very different tone from the surrounding skin. Burn Scar Camouflage is a specialized medical tattoo treatment designed to fill the lack of pigment and restore a more even and natural look to the area. At Sara Scalp in Jerusalem we specialize in this treatment, with a team trained in Brazil and Turkey.

Why Are Burn Scars Different from Regular Scars?

A burn damages the skin layers differently from a surgical cut. Melanocytes (the cells responsible for pigment production) are often destroyed, so the area remains light or white even years later. In addition, the texture may be different – smoother, wrinkled, or with changes in blood vessels. Therefore the camouflage technique for burn scars requires special skill in tone matching and delicate work on tissue that has already undergone trauma.

The treatment is suitable for burn scars that have already matured and stabilized. In every case we examine the condition of the skin, the depth of the original burn, and the current tone – and only then plan the series of treatments.

How Is Burn Scar Camouflage Performed at Sara Scalp?

The process begins with a comprehensive consultation in which we evaluate the area, photograph it in natural light, and explain the realistic possibilities. Then careful color matching is performed – sometimes several small tests are needed to reach the perfect shade. The work itself is done in very thin layers so as not to overload the skin and not create a “painted” look.

In most cases several sessions are required. Between sessions the color stabilizes and we see how it integrates with the skin. This allows gradual correction and improvement until the best result. The final outcome does not completely erase the scar (the texture remains), but dramatically reduces the color contrast – making the area far less noticeable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can all types of burns be treated? +
The treatment is mainly suitable for stable burn scars with pigment loss. Active burns or areas with infection are not suitable. In the consultation we will evaluate the specific case.
How many sessions are required? +
Usually 2–5 sessions, depending on the size of the area, the depth of the color change and the skin’s response. We will build a personal plan in the consultation.
Does the result look like a tattoo? +
No. The goal is the appearance of even skin, not a decorative tattoo. When the work is done correctly – those around you will not notice that a treatment was performed.
