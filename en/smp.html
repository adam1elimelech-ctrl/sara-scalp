Scalp SMP · Jerusalem

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP)

Tiny dots on the scalp that read as follicles in a short-crop look. We do not implant hair. We build density and a hairline that holds up close.

SMP is not a transplant

If you want length, this is the wrong treatment. If you want a clean buzzed look or to fill thinning inside short hair, it may be the right one.

Quality is judged in close-up: dot shape, spacing, and blend into existing hair. A hairline drawn too low looks drawn. We design it with you in the mirror.

Sessions and healing

Session one is a foundation — often about half the final density. Dots look dark and a little red. Days 1–4: keep the scalp dry. Softening in weeks two to four is planned, not a failure.

Most people need two or three sessions. Sun on a bare scalp in Jerusalem fades work faster without SPF once the skin has closed.
