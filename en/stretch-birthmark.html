Stretch marks and birthmarks are two different conditions, but both can significantly affect self-confidence. At Sara Scalp we offer dedicated medical tattooing for both – Stretch Mark Camouflage Tattooing and Birthmark Camouflage Tattooing – with a team trained in the techniques in Brazil and Turkey.

Medical Tattoo for Stretch Marks (Stretch Mark Camouflage)

Stretch marks form when the skin stretches rapidly – during pregnancy, weight gain or loss, or adolescence. They often appear as light or reddish lines and create contrast with the surrounding skin. Creams and laser can slightly improve texture, but the color difference is harder to close. Medical tattooing allows the marks to be filled with a precise tone matching your skin, so the contrast decreases and the area looks more even.

The treatment is suitable for stable stretch marks (usually white/silvery). We work in delicate layers, match the color in natural light, and build the result gradually over several sessions. The result is not 100% perfect (because it is living skin), but the improvement can be enormous – enough to feel comfortable in fitted clothes or a swimsuit.

Medical Tattoo for Birthmarks (Birthmark Camouflage)

Birthmarks can be in different shades – brown, reddish, or dark – and appear anywhere on the body. For many people, especially when the mark is large or in a prominent place (neck, face, arms), hiding it becomes a daily habit. Medical tattooing for birthmarks aims to reduce visibility by matching the tone to the surrounding skin or by filling the area with a blending color.

The work requires high precision, especially in sensitive areas or close to the face. We examine the type of mark, its depth, and the tone – and adapt the approach accordingly. The result allows many people to feel freer – to put their hair up, wear short sleeves, or simply not think about the mark all the time.

The Process at Sara Scalp

In both cases the process begins with consultation and assessment. We look at the area in natural light, explain what can be achieved, and tailor a plan. Then – color matching, layered application, and follow-up sessions as needed. Always with clear home-care instructions and support along the way.

Whether it is stretch marks after pregnancies or weight loss, or a birthmark that has accompanied you for years – we are here to help you feel more beautiful and confident. Our team brings international experience and personal attention to every client.
