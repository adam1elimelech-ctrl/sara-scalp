How Does the Medical Tattoo Process Work?

Every treatment at Sara Scalp begins with a personal and comprehensive consultation. We listen to your story, examine the treated area in natural light, and explain exactly what can be achieved. Then comes precise color matching – one of the most critical stages for treatment success. Work is performed in delicate layers with specialized medical tattoo needles, while maintaining full sterility.

In most cases several sessions are required to reach the optimal result. Between sessions you receive precise home-care instructions that support healing and color retention. The final result looks natural – not like a decorative tattoo, but like more even skin. Many clients report that people around them don’t notice a treatment was done, only that the area looks better.

We believe in full transparency: we will explain in advance how many sessions are expected, what the healing process involves, and what realistic results can be achieved. Every person is different, and every scar or pigmentation area requires a personalized approach. Therefore we do not promise “magic”, but professional, patient and precise work that brings significant improvement in appearance and self-confidence.

Who Is Medical Tattoo Suitable For?

Medical tattoo is suitable for a wide range of people. Those who had surgery (cesarean, aesthetic, orthopedic or other) and were left with a prominent scar; those who were burned and left with white or red discoloration; those who suffer from hypopigmentation (white areas on the skin); women after pregnancy or weight loss with stretch marks; people with birthmarks who want to reduce their visibility; those who want to add natural freckles or enhance their cheeks without injections; and men and women dealing with hair loss who seek a non-invasive aesthetic solution such as SMP.

Our treatments are also suitable for those who have already tried creams, laser or other treatments and did not receive a satisfactory result. Medical tattoo works on a different principle – it adds pigment into the skin itself, so the change is more stable. Of course, not every case is suitable for every treatment, which is why the initial consultation is so important. We examine skin type, scar or area condition, tone, and expectations – and only then recommend the appropriate plan.
