Cheek Enhancement with Permanent Makeup

A healthy, lifted and fresh look for the cheeks – without injections and without daily makeup

Book a Cheek Enhancement Consultation

Cheek Enhancement with permanent makeup (Cheek Enhancement / Permanent Blush) is a gentle and natural way to add color and volume to the cheeks. The result looks like your own skin – only healthier, more vital and slightly lifted. At Sara Scalp in Jerusalem we perform this treatment with high precision and full personal customization of shade and placement.

Why Choose Permanent Cheek Enhancement?

Many of our clients are looking for a fresh look without needing hyaluronic acid injections or daily makeup. Permanent cheek makeup gives exactly that: subtle lasting color, a slight lift to the appearance of the cheeks, and a sense of health and vitality. When the work is done correctly – no one notices you did anything, only that the face looks better.

How Is the Treatment Performed?

In the consultation we choose together the shade (soft pink, peach, or a tone that suits your skin) and the exact placement on the cheeks. Then the work is performed with fine needles, in thin layers. It is important not to overdo it – we build the color gradually. Sometimes a follow-up session is needed after a few weeks to reach the perfect result.

Healing is easy. Avoid strong sun and heavy makeup in the first days, and keep the area moisturized.

Advantages

  • Natural and healthy look
  • Time saved on daily makeup
  • Result that lasts months to years
  • No injections and no temporary fillers
  • Full personal customization of shade and placement

The secret to success is delicacy. At Sara Scalp we insist on a shade that does not look “made-up,” placement that suits the facial structure, and a result that looks like your own skin – only better.
