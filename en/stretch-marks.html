Stretch marks · Jerusalem

Stretch mark camouflage

White, shiny striae can be filled toward the surrounding skin tone. Red, new marks wait until they fade to silver.

Colour first, not a new abdomen

Camouflage reduces the white flash. The groove does not vanish. That limit is said at consult, not after two sessions.

Belly after pregnancy is the most common visit. Thighs, chest and arms are assessed the same way: settled colour, then layers along the lines — not a wash over the whole area.

Treatments that are not the same job

Laser and home devices may help texture or redness. They do not refill missing colour in a silver line. Creams help comfort. Surgery is a different path.

If another pregnancy is planned soon, say so. We time work between pregnancies when marks are white and stable.
