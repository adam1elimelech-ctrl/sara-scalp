מה חשוב לדעת וקשה למצוא במודעה

Real timing between sessions

We book the next layer when the skin has closed and colour has settled — often four to six weeks, not seven days on a calendar.

What is normal and what is not

Normal: pink days, temporary dark, light crust, itch, planned fade, slight patchiness for the next visit.

Not normal: fever, spreading red streaks, pus, pain that worsens after day three. Call a doctor and the clinic.

Medicines, pregnancy and scans

No new series in pregnancy. Tell an MRI operator you have pigment in the skin. That informs them; it does not automatically forbid a scan.

Days 1 to 14 — the pattern that repeats

Evening of day 0: pink and warm. Day 1: keep it as dry as directed. Days 2–4: gentle wash, pat dry, thin ointment. Days 5–10: flakes fall alone; colour looks weaker. Days 10–14: still no chlorine or scrub.

Questions that return after every treatment

Sleeping on the site: avoid hard pressure the first night. Work: most offices yes; dirty or sweaty sites need a plan. Children are not treated here.

Why you cannot copy aftercare from a group chat

Clinics abroad use different windows. The sheet from our room wins if it differs from this page.

Scar camouflage — extra detail before you book

A long tummy-tuck line is not a short caesarean. Keloid is not a flat scar. Hair over a line needs a shave plan.

Burns — what we check besides colour

Sensation. Grafts. Whether tightness or colour is the main complaint.

Hypopigmentation — the ‘a bit darker’ trap

The second session is not an excuse to flood. Stop early rather than go grey.

Stretch marks — the next pregnancy and weight

A planned pregnancy or a large weight swing mid-series can pause the work.

Birthmarks — the medical line

Any change in border, colour or height since your last doctor visit must be said. Camouflage does not replace derm follow-up.

SMP — a work week and evenings out

Office work is fine. A photoshoot on day two is not. Skip alcohol the same evening.

A short kit at home

The ointment we gave you. Mild soap. A clean towel. SPF after closure. Loose clothes. That is enough.

How to photograph progress

Same window, same distance, no filter. Day 0, 7 and 21.

If you skipped a rule

Sunburn on day four or a pool on day six: tell us. Silence does not fix it.

Fees, series and cancelling

Session count is an estimate after we look. Illness and a sudden rash: move the date. Treating unready skin is the expensive mistake.

One line per treatment

Scar: stable then layers. Burn: closed, slow, no scratch. White patch: stable, careful sun. Stretch marks: white lines, loose clothes. Birthmark: flat and quiet. SMP: dry first days, planned fade, more than one session.

Why this page is long on purpose

An ad does not contain day six. Read it so the consult is shorter and more precise.

Short answers by day

Shower the first evening: keep the site out of the blast. Soap: mild, no scrub. Moisturiser: after the crust has gone, if we said so. Walking: yes. Heavy sweat: later. Sea: after closure.
