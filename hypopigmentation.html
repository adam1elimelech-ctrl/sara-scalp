Hypopigmentation — refilling a white patch in Jerusalem

Hypopigmentation is local loss of colour. The skin may feel almost normal. We refill pigment in a stable patch. We do not treat spreading disease.

New or growing spots need a dermatologist first. A patch that has been unchanged for months can be assessed at Hamor 1.

Where hypopigmentation comes from

After inflammation, injury, certain procedures, or as a stable leftover of a skin condition. The label matters less than whether the patch is still changing.

How we check suitability

Daylight. Edge, size, stability, and what sits around it. Active vitiligo is not a tattoo problem.

What happens in the session

Conservative fill. Going too dark on a white patch leaves a grey island that is harder to live with than a slightly light one.

Face, hands and body

Hands and face take sun and wear. Body patches hide under clothes. The aftercare changes with the site.

After treatment

The site looks darker first, then softens. Do not pick. Itch is common; scratching pulls colour out. Follow the sheet from the room.

What this treatment does not do

It does not cure the disease that made the patch. It does not stop a new patch appearing nearby. If that happens, we stop.

Hypopigmentation versus scar camouflage

A scar has texture. A hypopigmented patch may not. Both are colour jobs. The consult still treats them as different maps.

Who does not start now

Red marks, pregnancy now, skin still stretching from a large weight change in progress.

Booking in Jerusalem

When the lines appeared, red or white, and a daylight photo.

A patch after psoriasis or inflammation

Only when the disease on that spot is quiet. We do not tattoo an active plaque.

After laser or a peel

Wait until the skin has truly settled. Fresh post-procedure pallor is not a camouflage candidate.

Several patches on one body

We prioritise what bothers you in the mirror. Not every spot must be treated.

Children and adults

Adults only here.

Sun in Jerusalem

The skin around a white patch tans. The patch then shouts again. Protection is part of the treatment.

The treatment day

Clean skin, no heavy cream. Colour mixed to the neighbour skin. A first layer that looks slightly light on purpose.

A year or two later

Sun and turnover fade work. A refresh is possible. Daily protection delays it.

What to write

When it started, whether it spreads, treatments already used, daylight photo.

Summary

Stable white patch. Conservative layers. Sun plan. Not a cure for active disease.

A patch that can start versus one that must wait

Unchanged for months, quiet edge: possible. New dots beside it, changing border: wait and see a doctor.

The fear it will stay grey

That is why the first layer stays light. Grey from overfill is the mistake we refuse on purpose.

Work, sea and clothes

Desk work is usually fine the next day. Sea after closure. Hands need a more careful week.

Medicines and other conditions

List what you take. Isotretinoin and active immune disease change timing.

Why not a standard permanent-makeup studio

A white medical patch is not a brow. Undertone mistakes show for years.

Privacy

Price without a blind list

Size and session count set the cost. We estimate after seeing the patch, not on the phone.

Travel to Jerusalem

Leave room between sessions for healing. Do not plan a week of full sun on a treated face.

After the series ends

Judge in the same daylight. A later refresh is a separate decision.

Questions that return at consult

Will it look painted. How many visits. What if a new patch appears. What sun does.

Legs versus hands

Hands work and wash more. Legs hide and tan in summer. Aftercare is not copied from one to the other.

When a patch sits next to a scar

Two textures, two colour problems. We decide what to treat first.

What not to send in the form

Heavy filters. Night photos. Ten pictures of the same flash. Two daylight frames are enough.

Practical close

Stability first. Light layers. Sun. Hamor 1 if the patch has been quiet.
