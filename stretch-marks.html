Full guide

Stretch mark camouflage with medical tattooing in Jerusalem

The white flash of a stretch mark can be brought closer to the surrounding skin. The groove does not disappear. That sentence is the brief.

Red versus white

Red marks wait. White marks can be assessed. Treating red lines too early wastes sessions.

Belly, thighs, chest and arms

Most visits are postpartum abdomen. Thighs and chest are the same logic: settled colour, then layers along the lines — not a wash over the whole area.

At the consult

When you gave birth or changed weight. Whether another pregnancy is planned. Daylight look at colour and density of lines.

The session and the layers

Work along the striae. Dense fields need more visits. The first pass looks darker, then settles.

After treatment

The site looks darker first, then softens. Do not pick. Itch is common; scratching pulls colour out. Follow the sheet from the room.

What stretch marks do not get from this

The skin does not become the abdomen from before pregnancy. Elasticity is not rebuilt with pigment.

Another pregnancy

Say if you are planning one. We time work between pregnancies when marks are white.

Who does not start now

Red marks, pregnancy now, skin still stretching from a large weight change in progress.

Privacy and price

Photos stay private unless you agree. Cost follows area and sessions after we see you.

Booking in Jerusalem

When the lines appeared, red or white, and a daylight photo.

First birth versus later births

More pregnancies can add lines. We treat what is white and stable now.

Men and weight change

The same rule: white settled marks. Shoulders and abdomen after gym or weight loss are common.

What you already tried

Oils and creams rarely refill colour. Devices may change texture. Tell us so we do not duplicate a plan.

Swimwear, belts and daily wear

Loose clothes for the first days. A tight waistband rubs a fresh belly field.

Sun on a belly in Jerusalem

A tanned abdomen next to treated lines brings contrast back. Cover or high SPF after closure.

Discomfort, time and work

Most people return to desk work quickly. Heavy core training waits a week or as directed.

What to write

When it started, whether it spreads, treatments already used, daylight photo.
