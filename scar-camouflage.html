Scar camouflage — a medical-tattoo guide in Jerusalem

Scar camouflage is paramedical tattooing: pigment placed in thin layers so a pale scar is less obvious next to the surrounding skin. It is not a decorative cover-up and it is not makeup.

At Sara Scalp in Jerusalem we match colour in daylight to the live skin beside the scar. The aim is that the eye no longer jumps to the line. Texture may still be felt. That limit is said before we start.

How scar camouflage actually works

Missing colour in a white or silver scar is rebuilt with medical pigment, in thin layers, not as a flood of ink in one sitting. Fibrous scar tissue often holds less pigment than normal skin, which is why more than one session is normal.

The first session looks darker than the finished result. Colour then softens as the surface heals. We judge the work after that settle — not the same evening.

Which scars are suitable

Settled, pale scars after surgery, a cut or an injury. Many people come after a caesarean, a tummy tuck, breast surgery or an orthopaedic line.

Red, hot or thickening scars are not treated yet. A scar under a year old is often too early. Keloid tendency is a reason to wait or refer.

Scar camouflage after a caesarean

A caesarean line sits under waistbands. Colour can be softened; the groove does not vanish. Plan loose clothing for the first days so the belt does not rub pigment out.

If another pregnancy is planned soon, say so at consult. Timing between pregnancies is part of the plan.

What happens at the consult

Daylight, not a phone flash. We look at colour, texture, maturity and what you actually want to see in a mirror. Photos you send help a first screen; the decision is in the room.

If the scar is not ready we say so. A declined start is part of the service.

How many sessions scar camouflage needs

Usually several, weeks apart. Scar tissue does not take colour like a tattoo on clear skin. Anyone promising one session for every scar is selling a slogan.

The gap between visits is set by healing, not by a calendar package.

Prep and the treatment day

Avoid a deep tan on the site. Tanned skin makes us match a colour that looks too dark when the tan drops. Skip extra alcohol the day before. Arrive in clean, loose clothes.

The site looks pink and stronger in colour that evening. That is expected.

After scar camouflage — how you keep the result

Clean hands. A thin layer of the ointment we give you. Do not pick flakes. Keep pools, saunas and hard sweat off the site in the first weeks.

Jerusalem sun on a pale scar will bring contrast back if you skip protection once the skin has closed.

Scar camouflage versus other treatments

Laser and revision surgery are different jobs. Creams help comfort. Camouflage fills missing colour. We do not pretend it replaces a surgeon.

Why this clinic in Jerusalem

Hamor 1. Colour mixed beside your scar, not from a stock chart. We would rather delay than tattoo a scar that is still changing.

Scar camouflage by body area

Abdomen, chest, face, limbs — each site has different rub, sun and clothing. A face scar is judged more slowly than a hip line. We say which constraints apply to your site.

What the weeks after feel like

Tightness, itch, a darker phase, then a fade. Unevenness before the next layer is common. Panic at day eight is usually the healing cycle, not a lost treatment.

Scar camouflage and skin tone

We match the skin next to the scar, not an ‘average Israeli tone’. Darker and lighter skin both need care with undertone so the fill does not turn grey or orange.

After mole or lesion removal

Only when the site is quiet and a doctor is happy with the scar. Changing borders are not a tattoo problem — they are a medical one.

After an accident or a cut

Jagged or wide injury scars are mapped piece by piece. One sitting rarely finishes an uneven field.

What to ask so you do not regret starting

What will still be visible close up. How many sessions are likely. What sun and clothing do to the site. What happens if you dislike the first layer.

Privacy and photographs

Photos you send are for advice. Nothing goes on the site without consent. Many people never appear in the gallery and that is fine.

Maintenance over the years

Sun and skin turnover fade pigment. A refresh after years is common. Daily SPF on an exposed scar is the cheapest maintenance.

Short summary before you book

Stable pale scar. Layers. Honest contrast drop, not a magic erase. Daylight consult at Hamor 1.

Day to day: clothes, sea and how it looks

After healing you dress as usual. Sea and pools wait until the surface has closed. People who know where the scar is may still find it close up. Strangers usually do not.

Who should not start now

Active scars, keloid risk, recent isotretinoin, pregnancy, infection on the site. If that is you, we wait.
